One engineer. Six disciplines.

Each one is honest about where it stops.

№ 04
01

Mechanical

Engine parts, gears, shafts, brackets, enclosures and assemblies. Fits and tolerances, manufacturability, overlap and mass checks.

Limit Checks use typical shop guidelines, not your shop’s limits.

02

Automotive

Wheel hubs, brake rotors, control arms, steering knuckles, pistons and flywheels.

Limit A recognised bolt pattern is not a fitment claim.

03

Aerospace

Airfoils, stability, engine cycle, mass and CG, with a signed review trail.

Limit Ends at “ready for engineering review”. Never certification.

04

Civil

Survey surfaces, alignments, earthworks, drainage and retaining wall stability.

Limit Screening level. The limits are yours to set.

05

Buildings

Layouts, structure, schedules, drawing sets and rule checks.

Limit Conceptual rules only. Never reports a full code pass.

06

Circuits

Place and route a board, then export a KiCad project and Gerbers.

Limit A light design-rule check. Run a full one before you order boards.

Questions, answered.

№ 08
01Will it tell me when it is not sure?
Yes. Every check answers PASS, FAIL or UNKNOWN. UNKNOWN means NitoCAD could not measure it or an input was missing, and it is never shown as a pass.
02Can it certify my design?
No. Aerospace results stop at “ready for engineering review”, civil checks are screening level and building rules are conceptual. A licensed engineer must verify anything that gets built.
03Does it change the numbers I type?
No. Every value you type is kept exactly as written. If NitoCAD sees a problem with a dimension, it flags it and leaves the value alone.
04Is the preview really free?
Yes. The preview is a free STL mesh and needs no wallet. Generating the STEP, IGES, DXF and PDF files uses credits, from 2 on Engine up to 40 with the strongest AI.
05What if a build fails?
You are not charged. A failed generation is refunded.
06Can I start from my own file?
Yes. Type the part in plain English, or attach a STEP file, a DXF or a sketch.
07Which formats can I export?
STEP, STL, IGES, DXF, PDF, GLB, 3MF and URDF.
08How do I pay?
Payments settle in native BOT on BOT Chain. One credit covers one generation. See full pricing.
